My project — real-time human tracking

PythonMediaPipeOpenCVWebcam

When I went on the PEARL research trip, I saw multiple types of sensors and lights that fascinated me. However, I didnt hear anything of actual human tracking in a 3d vector space. .Therefore, I built a system using python and mediapipe that watches through my webcam and draws a skeleton tracking system to track my movements. It started as a way to learn, and turned into one of the most interesting things I have made in my field of programming.

  • Real-time pose detection using MediaPipe's pose estimator and a standard webcam
  • Live skeleton overlay and video pipeline built with opencv
  • Debugged and tuned it myself plenty of trial and error (trust me), and an unthavomable amount of patience
